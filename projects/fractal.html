Fractal Imager

role
Solo project
focus
Mandelbrot · Julia · Newton · Perlin & Simplex noise

Fractal Imager is my sandbox for algorithmic art, born from a desire to see maths produce visceral colour. The tool blends classic fractal sets — Mandelbrot, Julia, Newton — with layered Perlin and Simplex noise so you can sculpt both rigid fractal silhouettes and organic textures in the same canvas.

A fractal is a mathematical pattern where you can zoom indefinitely and still see structures that resemble the whole, revealing infinite detail inside finite space.

A custom playground lets you dial in iteration counts, escape radius, colour palettes, and noise modulation while previewing the outcome in real time.
