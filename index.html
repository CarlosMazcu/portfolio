about

I'm Carlos Mazcuñán Blanes, a game programmer with an HND in Videogame Programming (ESAT). What really drives me is that moment when a technical problem that seemed impossible starts to make sense, when you find that elegant solution that makes everything click.

I specialize in gameplay and graphics, building robust and efficient systems in C++, Unreal Engine, and OpenGL. From rendering pipelines to internal tools, I like taking features from technical design to final polish. But code is only one part: my teammates often say I'm the one who keeps the good vibe in the team, and I believe communicating well and adapting to different ways of working is just as important as writing clean code. I'm comfortable working under pressure, and outside of work I keep coding — there's always a side project or a new technology I'm learning out of pure curiosity.
